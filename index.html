Task 1 · Reformulation

From everyday to clinical English

Keep the meaning and level of certainty unchanged. Use the support where useful; do not force every phrase into your answer.

Language support

Reporting symptoms

The patient reports… — patient-reported symptom

The patient describes… as… — reported severity

The patient denies… — negative history

Symptoms and findings

fatigue — tiredness

dyspnea — breathlessness

dyspnea on exertion — breathlessness with activity

edema — swelling (clinical finding)

dizziness — feeling dizzy

tachycardia — rapid heart rate

hypertension — high blood pressure

Useful patterns

has a history of…

has not yet been established

Examination findings

Examination reveals… — observed finding

…was observed on examination

No evidence of… — only if supported by examination

Time course and severity

acute / sudden onset

gradual onset

progressively worsening

intermittent / persistent

mild / moderate / severe

for five days / of five days’ duration

nocturnal — occurring at night

radiating to… — spreading to…

1. She says she gets out of breath when she walks upstairs.

2. The swelling in his ankles started about a week ago.

3. She has felt unusually tired for the past three months.

4. The pain gets worse when he takes a deep breath.

5. He says the headache comes and goes.

6. She has been coughing up blood since yesterday.

7. The doctor found that his blood pressure was low.

8. She says the pain spreads to her left shoulder.

9. His symptoms have become more severe over the last two days.

10. She felt dizzy when she stood up.

11. He has had a fever on and off since Monday.

12. The scan showed fluid around the lung.

Task 2 · Grammar in context

Modal meanings

Before you start

may / might / could — возможная причина, гипотеза (не подтверждение).

should — рекомендация или ожидаемое действие.

must not — категорически нельзя / недопустимо.

does not necessarily — не обязательно (не гарантирует).

In several contexts more than one modal may be grammatically possible. Choose the best option from the menu for the intended meaning.

Task 2 · Case 1 of 3

Case A · A new headache

A 29-year-old patient reports a new headache after a week of poor sleep. Neurological examination is normal. There is no fever or neck stiffness.

Complete the statements

1. Sleep deprivation be contributing to the headache.

2. A normal neurological examination exclude every serious cause of headache.

3. The clinician ask about red-flag symptoms before reaching a conclusion.

4. The headache be caused by meningitis; the current findings do not establish this.

Task 2 · Case 2 of 3

Case B · Persistent fatigue

A 41-year-old patient reports fatigue and reduced exercise tolerance. Blood tests show low hemoglobin and a low mean corpuscular volume. The cause has not yet been investigated.

Complete the statements

1. The blood results indicate a microcytic anemia.

2. Iron deficiency be one possible explanation, but other causes remain.

3. The clinician assume the cause is dietary without taking a history.

4. Further assessment be needed to identify the cause.

Task 2 · Case 3 of 3

Case C · Breathlessness after travel

A 54-year-old patient develops sudden shortness of breath after a long flight. Their heart rate is elevated. Oxygen saturation is lower than expected. The cause is unknown.

Complete the statements

1. The presentation indicate a potentially serious condition.

2. The clinician dismiss these symptoms as anxiety without assessment.

3. A pulmonary embolism be among the differential diagnoses.

4. The patient require urgent assessment.

Task 3 · Translation

Translate into clinical English

Use accurate clinical vocabulary and preserve uncertainty, negation and question focus.

1. Пациент сообщает об одышке при физической нагрузке.

2. Отёк лодыжек появился примерно пять дней назад.

3. Боль усиливается при глубоком вдохе.

4. Эти результаты могут указывать на воспалительный процесс.

5. Мы не должны считать этот диагноз подтверждённым.

6. Дефицит железа может быть одной из возможных причин.

7. Пациенту может потребоваться дополнительное обследование.

8. Нормальный результат не обязательно исключает заболевание.

9. Какой диагноз наиболее вероятен на основании этих данных?

10. Какое первоначальное обследование было бы наиболее целесообразным?
